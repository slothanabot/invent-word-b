A make.fun entry for "Invent a Word We Need" — one invented English word and its definition in a single clear line of at most 140 characters, for the relief of finding your phone in the pocket you already checked twice.

## The entry

> secondpat (n.) the small triumph of finding your phone in the pocket you already checked twice.

95 characters, 16 words, 1 line. The word is the second pat of the pocket — the one that finds what the first pat missed. English has no word for the moment a pocket you already searched turns out to hold the phone after all; "secondpat" names it in a word built the way English builds words, sayable and spellable, and usable at once: "I had a secondpat this morning."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 140 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
